## Task 4: Funding & Implementation Strategy

**4a. Document existing funding.** Document existing transit funding sources, including amounts, match requirements, and stipulations. Documentation should include federal, state, local, and private funding sources.

Deliverable: Fact sheet or report of existing funding

**4b. Develop funding model with stakeholder coordination and endorsement.** TRPA and some transit working group partners have initiated a draft regional transit funding model to document existing funding, begin determining costs across partners, and identify future funding needs. This funding model needs significant review and updates to formalize for use by TRPA and partners in administering regional transit funding. Coordinate with stakeholders, including the Tahoe Transportation District, the Tahoe Truckee Area Regional Transit, the South Tahoe Transit JPA, the South Shore Transportation Management Association, the Truckee North Tahoe Transportation Management Association, City of South Lake Tahoe, El Dorado County, Placer County, Douglas County, Washoe County, and the Town of Truckee. Working with stakeholders, develop strategies to include private ridership including non-federalized operators (ski shuttles, airport shuttles, Etc.). The updated funding model should be in a dynamic format that allows for future changes.

Deliverable: Updated Funding Model

**4c. Update Regional Transit Agreements and Administrative process.** Evaluate existing agreements and identify new agreements or MOUs to formally coordinate transit in Tahoe. This includes, but is not limited to operating agreements, interlocal agreements, funding agreements, data reporting requirements, funding allocation formulas, facility lease and use agreements, and other necessary agreements. Review TRPA's existing handbooks, policies, plans, and procedures.

Deliverable: Inventory of existing agreement and requirements

Deliverable: Recommend agreements or MOU updates
