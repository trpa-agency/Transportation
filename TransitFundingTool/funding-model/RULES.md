# Rules applied by the funding model

Generated by `tools/rules_doc.py` from the rule registry in `engine/funding-engine.js` and the source data in `data/seed.json`. Each rule ID appears in the code where the rule is applied and in every check result that cites it.

Severity applies to rules that are checked against an allocation: an **error** breaks a required rule, cap, or match requirement; a **warning** flags a use outside a source's stated intent; **info** reports a gap or an unallocated amount.

## Rules-based allocation (optimizer)

Code: `buildLP, simplex, certify, explainUnused`

| ID | Rule | Type | What the model does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OPT-01 | Objective | Optimizer | The rules-based allocation maximizes funded service: the sum over services of funds applied, never above cost. | Model method |
| OPT-02 | Constraints | Optimizer | Each source applies no more than its amount; only uses that pass every required rule (ELIG-01 to ELIG-07) are allowed, within eligible portions; no service receives more than its cost; match is met on every service (MATCH-03). With no over-funding and match met, the federal caps (FED-02, FED-03) hold automatically. | Model method |
| OPT-03 | Funds stay with the receiving agency | Optimizer | By default a source is applied only to its receiving agency's services. With interagency transfers allowed, it may fund other agencies' services at a small penalty, each flagged under PREF-04. | Model setting |
| OPT-04 | Preferences | Optimizer | Uses that break a preference (PREF-01 to PREF-03) are allowed but avoided (OPT-06), so they occur only when they fund service, or spread a shortfall, that the preferred uses cannot. In strict mode they are not allowed. | Model setting |
| OPT-05 | Shortfalls spread evenly | Optimizer | When funds cannot cover every service, the engine keeps total funded service at its maximum and then raises the lowest share of cost funded on any service as high as the rules allow, so gaps are spread rather than concentrated. This setting can be turned off. | Model method (lexicographic, max-min coverage) |
| OPT-06 | Tie-breaks | Optimizer | Among allocations that fund the same service equally evenly, the engine applies fares first, then federal funds, then funds earmarked to specific services, then time-limited funds, and holds back flexible recurring funds; uses that break a preference are avoided. Tie-breaks never reduce funded service or evenness. | Model method (lexicographic optimization) |
| OPT-07 | Proof of optimality | Optimizer | Each solution is checked for primal feasibility, dual feasibility, and zero duality gap on funded service. A solution that fails is reported, not used silently. | Model method |
| OPT-08 | Why funds go unused | Optimizer | An unused amount is explained as: no eligible service, eligible services already fully funded, not enough non-federal match, or an eligible-portion cap. | Model method |
| OPT-09 | Each year solved separately | Optimizer | The rules-based allocation is solved independently for each fiscal year. | Model method |
