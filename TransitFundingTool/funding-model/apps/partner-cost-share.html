Beta
Jurisdiction shares are placeholders for most services: an equal split among the jurisdictions in the costs tab. Use the formula builder to set them from measured splits of miles, hours, stops, and ridership, the method in the SSTMA working model. Funding is the rules-based allocation for the year shown (OPT-01 to OPT-09).

Cost and local share by jurisdiction

Each jurisdiction's share of service cost, the federal, state, private, and fare funding applied to that share, and the local share left, against what the jurisdiction contributes (RES-03).

Cost-share formula builder

Enter each jurisdiction's percent of a service's miles, hours, stops, and riders. The share is the weighted average, as in the SSTMA Route 19, 22, and 28 worksheet.

A regional formula for local shares

Divides the region's total local share among jurisdictions by one basis and compares the result with what each contributes today.

Partner detail

Services attributed to one jurisdiction and the sources it contributes.

Funding by commitment

How firmly each agency's revenue is committed for the year shown.
